# ACTIVE — design context (for Impeccable PRODUCT.md / DESIGN.md)

## Product
Personal health & active-life dashboard for one athlete: four sites (HEALTH, RUNNING, CYCLING, HIKING) plus the active-watch operations hub. Mode: **Operate / Read** — the visitor scans their own training and recovery data and understands changes. Brand: ACTIVE — PRECISION MOTION. Dark navy is the house surface; light is the same system on paper.

## People
The owner, on a laptop or phone, reviewing their own COROS-synced data: did recovery follow the training? what changed this week? where did I go? Density and legibility outrank persuasion; no marketing copy, no conversion funnel.
